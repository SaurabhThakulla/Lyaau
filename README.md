# Lyaau

### Local shopping. Delivered to you.

**Lyaau** is a local multi-vendor e-commerce platform being built for **Tikapur, Nepal**.

The goal is simple: connect customers with local shops and make it easy to discover, order, and receive products from businesses around them.

## What is Lyaau?

Lyaau brings products from different local businesses into one platform.

Customers can browse and order products such as:

* Groceries & daily essentials
* Clothes & fashion
* Herbs & local products
* Cosmetics & personal care
* Electronics & accessories
* Household products
* Other products from local businesses

Merchants can list their products on Lyaau, receive online orders, and reach more customers without having to build their own e-commerce platform.

## How it works

```text
Customer
   ↓
Browse products
   ↓
Place order
   ↓
Merchant prepares order
   ↓
Freelance rider accepts delivery
   ↓
Customer receives order
```

## Business Model

Lyaau will initially use a combination of:

* Product-level markup
* Delivery fees
* Sponsored products
* Merchant advertising

The platform will start with **Tikapur** and expand to other locations after validating the local marketplace and delivery model.

## Technology

* **Frontend:** React Native / Expo
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS / NativeWind
* **Backend:** Node.js + TypeScript
* **Database:** PostgreSQL
* **Cache:** Redis
* **API:** REST
* **Deployment:** Docker + VPS

## Project Status

🚧 **Early Development**

The initial focus is building and validating the core marketplace experience in Tikapur before expanding to additional cities and categories.

## Vision

> **Make local shopping simple, accessible, and fast.**

Lyaau aims to become a digital marketplace connecting local businesses with customers, starting from Tikapur and gradually expanding across Nepal.
